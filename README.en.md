**From concept to shootable screenplay, from rough draft to precise diagnosis — a complete story pipeline. The methodology is not assembled writing tips: it is the operationalization of the author's four self-authored writing theories (*Characters*, *Fate*, *Story Evolution*, *Literary Evolution*).**

```
Concept → Original Writing Flow (a 7-stage protocol that grows a shootable script)
Novel   → Adaptation Flow (keep the narrative function, replace the information carrier)
Draft   → Fiction Critique (red/yellow-card diagnosis + W-value scoring)
Script  → Performance Video Prompts (what to play, how to play, what not to play)
```

## Have you ever run into these?

- A **great concept** in your head, but no idea where to start
- A finished novel that feels **"somewhere off"**, but you can't pinpoint the chapter or the character
- Adapting a novel and ending up with a **"translated novel"** instead of a movie
- Video prompts that always lack a sense of **"living person"**
- Endings that feel **"over"** instead of **"not over"**

This kit's answer is not "read more, write more" — it is **verifiable criteria** from four writing theories: every problem can be located to a rule, a piece of evidence, and a fix.

---

## The Four Skills

### 📖 novel-to-screen-adaptation — Adaptation Flow (Novel → Screenplay)

> Adaptation is not preserving the original sentence. It is preserving the narrative function behind it and replacing the information carrier.

Proposition → Driver → Screen Objects → Scene Cards → Beat Sheets → a shootable screenplay. Hard rules at every step (action ≥40%, dialogue ≤3 lines, no unshootable words), director-mode driven, signed off at the end.

### ✍️ novel-original-writing — Original Writing Flow (Concept → Screenplay)

> Turn an idea into a situation people keep asking about — low entrance, high experience.

Seed stage (eight desires × six+one changes × low-entrance/high-experience) → sparrow worldbuilding → the five question sets (private/circumstance/conviction/action/becoming — 38 questions) → 24 solar terms structure → scene cards → performance injection → aftershock ending. 20 quality gates, co-created with the director.

### 🔍 novel-review — Fiction Critique (Draft → Diagnosis)

> A review answers "what's wrong, which theory it violates, where the evidence is, how to fix it" — not "do I like it".

Four-layer scan (character / structure / ending / prose) + red/yellow-card severity + **W-value scoring** (inherits the formula $W_{Story}=[(T\times\frac{M\cdot V\cdot \eta}{R^2})-S]\times e^{\xi}$, with criteria anchored to the current theories). Every conclusion traces back to a theory entry.

### 🎭 video-prompt-adapter — Performance Video Prompts (Script → Screen)

> A great video prompt is a director's performance note: what to play, how to play it, and what not to play.

Character interior / performance psychology / micro-performance vocabulary / negative constraints — performance-driven, not scene description.

## Why This Kit Is Different (Theory Highlights)

- **24 solar terms acceptance sentences**: "This section reads like midsummer, but the crack was never exposed" — pacing becomes a checkable criterion
- **Private/Circumstance/Conviction/Action/Becoming — 38 questions**: the same premise grows completely different stories when you swap the "private self"
- **19 missed-rhythm red lines**: "talking too much when it should hide", "unwilling to cut when it should kill" — problems get caught one by one
- **The Great Snow gate**: endings leave an aftershock under the snow instead of explaining everything — "a true aftershock is not a cliffhanger; it is meaning that hasn't been used up"
- **W-value scoring**: M·V·η product, R² squared-kill denominator, ξ decides the ceiling — a good story has a physical definition

## Quick Start

```text
"Adapt this novel into a screenplay" → Adaptation Flow
"Use the original writing flow for this concept" → Original Writing Flow
"Review this novel — where are the problems" → Fiction Critique
"Write me a video prompt" → Performance Prompts
```

**Option 1: Plugin marketplace (recommended)**

```text
/plugin marketplace add juemin4-source/novel-skills
/plugin install novel-skills@novel-skills
```

**Option 2: Manual copy**

Copy the four directories side-by-side into your project's `.claude/skills/` (or user-level `~/.claude/skills/`) — keep them at the same level, since skill-to-skill references are relative paths.
